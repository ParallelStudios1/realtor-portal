About the product: Realtor Portal is an agent-facing BACK OFFICE deal
management app (not IDX — there is no public listing search or display).
A subscribed agent opens one of their own private deals, enters an FMLS
listing number, and the property record fills itself from the feed:
address, list price, beds/baths, square footage, status, listing agent and
office, and the primary photo. That data is visible only to the
participants of that private deal.

Compliance notes:
- Listings with "Allow Internet Display" set to No are refused and never
  shown (we honor the flag at lookup time).
- Listing agent attribution (name and office) is displayed on every house
  record sourced from FMLS.
- No listing content is exposed publicly or outside the subscribing firm.
